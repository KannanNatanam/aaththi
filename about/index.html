Our story

Connecting You To What You Love.

Named after the aaththi flower

Aaththi takes its name from the aaththi flower (Bauhinia racemosa), a flower celebrated in classical Tamil literature and remembered in the title of Avvaiyar’s Aathichoodi. Our gold flower logo is drawn from it: simple, graceful and full of meaning.

What we make

We design party decorations and gifts for the moments people remember: a baby’s first birthday, a gender reveal, a newlywed home, Father’s Day, or a thank-you for a favourite teacher. Each product is designed by our own team, with a focus on clean design, good materials and a touch of humour.

Why we sell on Amazon

Every Aaththi product is fulfilled by Amazon. That means fast delivery, secure checkout and Amazon’s customer service and returns, while we focus on designing products you’ll love.
