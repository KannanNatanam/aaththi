Our name

Named after the aaththi flower

Aaththi takes its name from the aaththi flower (Bauhinia racemosa), celebrated in classical Tamil literature and remembered in Avvaiyar’s Aathichoodi. Like the flower, our products are meant to bring a little beauty and warmth to everyday moments.
